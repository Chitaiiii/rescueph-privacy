Delete your RescuePH account

RescuePH · developed by Miguel Angelo Solis Hidalgo

This page explains how to request deletion of your RescuePH account and the personal data associated with it, and what happens to that data afterwards.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to mashidalgo100@gmail.com with the subject “RescuePH account deletion request”.
  2. Send it from the email address registered to your RescuePH account, so the request can be matched to the right account.
  3. Include the mobile number on the account if you have it to hand. This is optional and only used to confirm the match.
  4. You will receive confirmation once the account has been deleted. We respond within 30 days, as required by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

You may also use the same address to ask for a copy of your data, or to correct it, instead of deleting it.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profile: name, username, email, mobile number, home address, date of birthDeleted
Identity verification images: government ID and selfieDeleted
Device push notification tokenDeleted
Incident report photographs you uploadedDeleted
Your sign-in credentialsDeleted, and you can no longer sign in

What may be kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Emergency records you sent: time, location, services requested and response timesKept for up to 2 years from the date of the emergency, because a station or local government unit may be required to account for how a response was handled. These records are disassociated from your profile.
Chat and support messagesKept for up to 1 year.
Crash and diagnostic logsRetained by Firebase Crashlytics under Google’s own retention policy. These do not identify you by name.

If you want these retained records deleted as well, say so in your email and we will delete them unless a station is legally required to keep them. Where that applies, we will tell you which records are affected and why.
