RescuePH — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Data controller: Miguel Angelo Solis Hidalgo, an individual developer based in the Philippines. RescuePH is not currently operated by, or endorsed by, any local government unit.

Privacy and deletion requests: mashidalgo100@gmail.com

Who this applies to

  • Residents who request emergency help or report non-urgent incidents.
  • Responders who are assigned to an emergency by a station.
  • Station and administrator staff who review requests and assign responders.

This policy covers the RescuePH Android application and the station web portal.

Precise location, specifically

  • Your location is read when you open the SOS flow so you can review it before anything is sent. Opening the SOS screen sends nothing.
  • When you send an emergency request, your location is attached to it and becomes visible to the receiving station and to the responder they assign.
  • While an emergency is active, your location may continue to update so a responder can find you.
  • Location is also used to show weather and local advisories.
  • You can refuse the location permission. The app will still run, but it cannot send an accurate emergency request without it.

Who your information is shared with

  • Local emergency stations and the responder assigned to you. This is the purpose of the app. An emergency request is only useful if the people responding can see it.
  • Station alert devices. Stations may operate hardware alert devices that poll for incoming emergencies in their area.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics), which stores and transports the data on our behalf.
  • Map, routing and information providers: OpenStreetMap (map tiles), OSRM (routing and arrival estimates), Nominatim (place names), Open-Meteo (weather), and the news sources shown in Updates. These receive the coordinates needed to answer the request.

We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

A limitation we state openly. Emergency records are currently readable without authentication so that station alert devices can poll them. This is a deliberate compatibility measure for existing hardware and is being migrated to authenticated device access. Until that migration completes, an emergency record should be treated as visible to anyone who knows the project identifier.

How long we keep it

  • Account and verification data (including ID images and selfie): while your account exists, and for 30 days after you delete it.
  • Emergency records: 2 years, because a station or local government unit may be required to account for a response.
  • Chat and support messages: 1 year.
  • Crash diagnostics: as retained by Firebase Crashlytics under Google’s policy.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) you may ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct anything wrong, delete your account and associated personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of your data, or withdraw consent at any time.

Write to mashidalgo100@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, verification images and device token. Records of emergencies you sent may be retained for the period above where a station is required to keep them.

Security

  • Access is controlled by Firebase Authentication and server-side security rules.
  • Verification images are stored so that only you and authorised reviewers can read them.
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at mashidalgo100@gmail.com if you believe your account has been compromised.

Children

RescuePH is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it. Nothing here prevents anyone, at any age, from calling emergency services directly.

The accessibility service

RescuePH includes an optional accessibility service used only to detect the volume button sequence that triggers Silent SOS, so an emergency can be sent with the screen off. It does not read your screen content, and it is not used for any other purpose. You can leave it disabled; Silent SOS will simply not work.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
